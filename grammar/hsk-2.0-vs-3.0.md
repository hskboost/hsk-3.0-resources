# HSK 2.0 vs HSK 3.0 Levels and Vocabulary

## HSK 3.0 Vocabulary Count by Level

HSK 2.0 has six levels, while HSK 3.0 adds a combined Levels 7–9 syllabus and assessment above Levels 1–6.

| Level | HSK 2.0 cumulative vocabulary target | HSK 3.0 imported syllabus entries at this level | HSK 3.0 imported entries through this level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 150 | 301 | 301 |
| 2 | 300 | 204 | 505 |
| 3 | 600 | 507 | 1,012 |
| 4 | 1,200 | 1,020 | 2,032 |
| 5 | 2,500 | 1,638 | 3,670 |
| 6 | 5,000+ | 1,815 | 5,485 |
| 7–9 | — | 5,622 | 11,107 |

The HSK 2.0 column reports official cumulative study targets. HSK 3.0 columns count entries in HSKBoost’s imported 2025 syllabus, including entries assigned to more than one level. These are entry counts, so a level can include multiple entries with the same spelling.
